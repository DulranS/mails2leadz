# Balancr — Business Value

*by Syndicate Software Solutions*

## What It Does

Balancr is a complete financial system for small businesses. It tracks wholesale orders through to receipt, purchases and service delivery costs, sales, inventory, unit economics, employee payroll, loans, recurring revenue and expenses, customers, projects, time tracking, fixed assets, opportunities, and generates professional financial statements — all in one place.

## Why It Matters

**You stop guessing about money.**
- See your entire financial picture in one place
- Know which products/services actually make money with item-level profitability
- See which branch is actually earning its keep: profit by location, with branch-specific costs tagged to the branch and shared overhead left unallocated rather than guessed at
- Understand your real profit margins (gross, operating, and net)
- Track cash flow, not just revenue — see when inventory purchases hit cash vs. when sales hit profit
- View three standard financial statements: Income Statement, Cash Flow Statement, and Balance Sheet
- Record tax payments you've actually made, netted against what the Balance Sheet says you still owe — no filing, no external tax integration, just your own accurate running total
- Tap-to-reveal glossary definitions for accounting terms (COGS, gross profit, accrual basis, etc.)
- Track customer lifetime value, acquisition cost, and payback periods
- See combined receivables and payables aging with payment tracking

**You never run out of stock.**
- Automatic reorder alerts based on your actual sales data
- Know exactly how much to order using Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) — minimizes ordering and holding costs
- See which items have stopped selling and how much cash is sitting on the shelf in them (slow stock: 90+ days with no sale), at cost, so you can discount, bundle or stop reordering
- Real-time stock level tracking across all products
- Track wholesale orders from placement through delivery before they arrive
- Units already on order are subtracted from reorder checks to avoid double-flagging
- Apply batch discounts or markups to multiple products at once (seasonal sales, wholesale deals)
- Create composite products with recipe-based costing (e.g., a meal made from multiple ingredients)

**You make better decisions.**
- Know your break-even point and overhead coverage ratio
- See contribution margin per sale
- Understand unit economics with variable costs per transaction
- Forecast revenue based on historical trends (linear regression + moving average), plus a cautious seasonal view once there are 24 months of history; batch trace (which batch each sale came from, who received a batch)
- Strategic financial health metrics: growth rates, liquidity ratios, profitability ratios, cash conversion cycle, leverage ratios, customer acquisition costs, and efficiency metrics
- Cash runway — how many months you can operate at your current burn rate
- Foreign-currency exposure: how much of what you are owed or owe is in another currency, and what a 5/10/20% move would do to it (booked rates, plus a free daily reference rate shown as a what-if; the books never change)
- Supplier price watch: items whose latest delivery cost 5%+ more, what it does to your margin, whether it is the supplier or the exchange rate, and a cheaper supplier if one exists
- What to order now: items at their reorder point with a suggested quantity, supplier, cost and per-supplier total, against the cash that is safe to commit
- Price what-if: profit effect of a price change and the volume you could lose before you are worse off
- Tax rate per item (exempt, zero-rated or a different rate) with the shop rate as the default; invoices tax each line at its own rate
- Budget by category: a monthly budget for each expense category against actual spend
- Late payment charge (optional, advisory): the charge built up on an overdue invoice, mentioned in the reminder
- Wholesale prices: a one-tap wholesale price for customers marked wholesale
- One-tap order drafts from the "What to order now" list
- Markup % shown alongside margin % to avoid pricing mistakes (50% markup ≠ 50% margin)
- Payroll-to-revenue ratio — see if labor costs are eating the business
- Score and rank opportunities with upside vs. cost analysis
- Before committing to a big purchase, see how much cash is actually safe to put to work (after tax and bills you owe and a safety buffer), then stress-test the decision as downside, as-planned and strong cases so a bet that only works in the best case is visible before any money moves
- Track projects with milestones, budgets, and financial summaries

**You save time.**

- Track recurring revenue and expenses automatically (rent, subscriptions, retainer clients)
- Employee payroll booked automatically as recurring expenses
- Loan payments calculated and split between principal/interest automatically
- Import/export your data easily via CSV
- One-click full backup — zip all your data into a single file
- First-run checklist guides you through initial setup
- Works on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop
- Offline banner shows when you're working without connection (data syncs when back online)
- Track time with clock in/out and automatic duration calculation
- See per-person utilization (billable vs. total hours clocked, trailing 30 days) to spot when a busy team isn't actually a billable one
- View searchable audit log of all changes (owner-only)

- Track accounts receivable aging at a glance
- Set an optional credit limit per customer — warned before you extend more credit to someone already over it (a heads-up, never a lock)
- Automatic overdue payment alerts
- See which customers owe you and for how long
- Print a one-page statement of account for any customer who owes you: every open invoice, what is overdue and the total, ready to hand over or save as PDF

- Combined receivables and payables view with payment tracking

- Unified system for hybrid businesses (selling products and billing labor)

- Services: labor/time-based with cost entries logged like wholesale purchases

**You track debt and capital properly.**
- Model loans with automatic amortization schedules
- Interest expense flows to Income Statement, principal repayment to Cash Flow
- Track initial investment, reinvestment, and owner withdrawals separately from operating P&L
- See payback progress and net cash position alongside monthly profit
- Track fixed assets with depreciation (straight-line, or reducing balance for equipment that wears out fast) and book value
- Grow capital in shares, bonds, funds or deposits: cost, value now, gains, income and yearly return, with a what-could-it-be-worth-later view that you set the assumptions for

- Investments flow through cash flow, profit and the balance sheet, so the books stay balanced
- Lend money and earn interest: what is owed, interest earned, who is late and for how long, what is still to come, and whether lending beats what the cash could earn elsewhere; interest counts as profit only when received
- Chase money you are owed from one ranked list (customer invoices and loans you made): who is late, how late, who is chasing them, what they last said, any promise to pay and when to follow up. Broken promises rise to the top, a one-tap WhatsApp reminder is ready written, and a per-collector scoreboard shows what each field officer has assigned, late and collected this month
- See stock over time: what inventory was worth at each past month-end, cash tied up in items that stopped selling, and items about to run out

**You understand your customers.**
- Customer database with lead source tracking
- Customer unit economics: lifetime value, acquisition cost, payback period, profit per customer
- See which customers are most profitable and which cost the most to acquire
- Track customer retention and repeat purchase patterns
- Expiry watch for perishable stock: expiry date and batch on each purchase, a warning of what is expired or running out of date (30/60/90 days) with its value at cost, and a one-step write-off
- Customer deposits and prepayments held as a liability (not income) until delivered, used on invoices or refunded, with the balance sheet and cash flow staying correct
- Repeat-income health for subscriptions, retainers and rent received: yearly run-rate, what started and ended last month, % lost, share of income that repeats, and whether it covers regular costs

**You manage projects effectively.**
- Track projects with milestones, budgets, and cost segments
- See project financials: revenue, costs, profit, and completion status
- Link time entries and expenses to specific projects
- Print project summaries for clients or internal review
- Sales tax summary split by rate, with exempt and zero-rated turnover shown
- Quantity price breaks offered as a one-tap suggestion on the sale form
- Serial number / IMEI and warranty tracking: find a sale by serial, see warranty status, print it on the receipt
- Bill the late charge on an overdue invoice with one tap (only what has not been billed before)
- One login for several businesses: an accountant, operator or multi-venture manager switches between the businesses they have been invited to
- Physical stock count: type what is on the shelf for every item and record all differences at once
- Dropshipping and made-to-order: buy the missing units for an order as you sell it, with the sale costed exactly
- Customer order frequency: how often each customer orders, who is due to reorder and who has gone quiet
- Net profit margin target: set the margin you are aiming for and see each recent month against it, with a warning after two misses in a row
- Pipeline expected value at the business's own win rate, and a customers x orders each x order size breakdown of revenue growth
- Playbook: written procedures with a review schedule (an overdue review rises to the top) and contract dates with notice periods (flagged as the notice window starts, so nothing renews or lapses by surprise); printable for staff without a login
- Bulk lead import: load a whole prospect list as new leads from a spreadsheet, duplicates skipped, ready for outreach and follow-up
- Stress test: apply a fall in sales or a rise in costs to the last three months and see profit, break-even, margin of safety and cash runway
- Marketing by channel: what each channel (search, social, events, print, email, referral...) cost against the leads, new customers, sales and profit it brought, with cost per lead, cost per new customer and what came back per 1 spent, so money goes where it pays
- Find prospects: businesses of one kind in one city or district from the free OpenStreetMap, reviewed before anything is added (duplicates of leads and customers marked), added as new leads; no paid map key, no deposit, nothing contacted
- Write with AI: a first message or follow-up drafted for each lead from what the business sells, and three post ideas on demand; you read and send them, every lead message gives an easy way to say no, one AI message per press
- Sales below cost: which items and customers lose money after discounts, over the last 90 days, so the business does not price itself to the bottom
- Tailor your menu: the owner hides the pages the business does not use (or starts from shop, service, both or just the basics), so a new business or a new hire sees a short menu instead of 43 items; nothing is deleted and everything stays one search away
- Bookings: appointments and reservations for a time, a seat or a person, with clashes refused, a week strip, no-show and cancellation rates, deposits held, and seasonal demand once there is a year of history
- Delegation: work handed to team members or freelancers, with due dates, urgent flags, overdue work first, and who is carrying what
- Freelancer and contractor attendance: their hours logged and costed by name, with no login needed
- Weekly scorecard: this week so far against last week for sales, leads, customers, support and feedback, with overdue follow-ups and waiting cases, so the owner can check the week in ten seconds
- Customer feedback score: the one-question "would you recommend us, 0 to 10?" logged per customer, with the score against the previous 90 days, low scores to follow up, recent buyers still to ask, and happy customers to ask for an introduction; honest about small samples
- Customer support log: complaints, questions, refund and warranty requests tracked to resolution, with open, urgent and waiting counts, average days to resolve, and customers who keep coming back
- Ready-written messages for leads, customers and support cases (first hello, follow-up, quote chase, win-back, thank-you, referral ask), opened in WhatsApp or email to edit and send; first contact always gives an easy way to say no
- Qualified lead stage: a lead is vetted (budget, authority, need, timeline) before a quote is spent on it
- Payslips with overtime, allowances, commission, no-pay days and EPF, printable; overtime and allowances can be recorded once as an expense
- Staff leave: paid and no-pay leave logged against a yearly allowance, with who is over it
- Commission on profit instead of sales, so pay lines up with what the business keeps
- Pipeline coverage: how many times the open leads cover what is still needed to reach this month's revenue target (about 3x is the usual safe level), so a thin funnel shows before the month is lost
- What if interest rates rise: the extra monthly payment and extra interest on the loans if the rate went up 1, 3 or 5 points, for loans the lender can reprice
- Routines: a fixed list of money habits (cash count, chasing overdue customers, bills, bank, stock count, month close), each checked against the records, so the owner can see at a glance what has slipped
- Operator health check: which customer accounts are overdue-heavy, short of cash, loss-making or gone quiet
- Record a billing milestone in the books with one tap: it becomes a real sale of a service (taxed, tagged to the project, payment recorded if already paid), and the project warns if the schedule shows more billed than the books hold
